How it works

From idea to classroom in four steps

  1. 1

    You share

    Fill out the short request form. Tell me the classroom problem, not just the feature.

  2. 2

    I review

    I read every request and reach out by email if I have questions or ideas.

  3. 3

    I build

    I design and build your tool, then send you a link to test it.

  4. 4

    You teach

    Use it with your students. It runs in any browser, no installs or accounts.

About

Hi, I'm Henry Yu.

I'm an Assistant Professor of Educational Technology at Teachers College, Ball State University. I teach future teachers how to use technology and AI in thoughtful, practical ways.

Teachers often know exactly what tool would help their students, but there's no time or budget to make it. That's where I come in. I build small, focused tools that solve real classroom problems, and I share them freely.
